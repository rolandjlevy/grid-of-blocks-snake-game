How to play

  • Pink is you, the snake
  • Blue is food: eat it to grow
  • Faded squares are obstacles: don't hit them, or yourself

Steer with the arrow keys. On a phone or tablet, swipe or use the arrow buttons. Edges wrap around to the other side.

Each food is worth 1 point, and your best score is saved on this device.

After a crash, press Enter or Space, or tap the board, to play again.
